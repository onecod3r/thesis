## 2. First camera test: sentences fail, isolated signs work (TODO §12.8)

The user reported that most sentences are garbage, with missing, extra and wrong glosses, although single
signs are recognized well. Nothing was trained. The robustness probes
(`gislr.3.streaming.live-robustness.ipynb`) run C1 + D3 over 400 evaluation-signer streams under
live-camera distortions:

- **Low fps** (the app's clock repeats frames): GER 0.293 → 0.504, mostly missed signs; isolated
  accuracy 0.737 → 0.719.
- **Jitter 1%**: 0.516, mostly extra signs; isolated 0.730.
- **No reset over 6 chained sentences**: 0.415; recall falls along the chain.
- **Landscape framing** (x × 0.56): 0.651 / isolated 0.416. **Mirror**: 0.990.
- The app requests 640×480 landscape. GISLR's framing is portrait and close: shoulders span 0.57 of the
  image width.
- **Fixes, no retraining:** interpolation 0.365; EMA α 0.5 0.337; per-session reframe 0.334 on aspect and
  scale (+0.04 on clean). The combined live-like case goes 0.592 → 0.481.

Report: `docs/reports/live-streaming-gap.md`. Questions to the user: the fps shown, the mirror setting,
the distance from the camera. Decisions owed: app fixes, a landmark recorder for real sentences, C1 v2
with normalized input + augmentation.

## 5. Sign patterns per phonological parameter (user: "start on the sign pattern first")

Research note B1 + B2, no training.

- Mapped GISLR's glosses to ASL-LEX 2.0: 229/250 (209 exact, 20 same-sign synonyms). The data is in
  `data/external/asl_lex/` (CC BY-NC 4.0).
- Added handshape, orientation, location, movement and signtype variables (left hand mirrored).
- Tested whether clips group by parameter value across different signs, gloss-disjoint.

Results:
- Every parameter passes (p < 0.005). Selected fingers 0.81 (chance 0.17); repeated movement 0.80;
  ulnar rotation 0.75; major location 0.65 (chance 0.25). The grid is near-diagonal: each family predicts
  its own parameter.
- With these variables, whole-sign templates go from 4.9% to 38.8% top-1, and separable glosses from 16%
  to 70%. Handshape alone gives 26.1%.
- Weak spots: sign type and movement shape.
