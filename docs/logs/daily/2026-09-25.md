## 5. Sign patterns per phonological parameter (user: "start on the sign pattern first")

Research note B1 + B2, no training.

- Mapped GISLR's glosses to ASL-LEX 2.0: 229/250 (209 exact, 20 same-sign synonyms). The data is in

- Added handshape, orientation, location, movement and signtype variables (left hand mirrored).
- Tested whether clips group by parameter value across different signs, gloss-disjoint.

Results:
- Every parameter passes (p < 0.005). Selected fingers 0.81 (chance 0.17); repeated movement 0.80;
  ulnar rotation 0.75; major location 0.65 (chance 0.25). The grid is near-diagonal: each family predicts
  its own parameter.
- With these variables, whole-sign templates go from 4.9% to 38.8% top-1, and separable glosses from 16%
  to 70%. Handshape alone gives 26.1%.
- Weak spots: sign type and movement shape.
